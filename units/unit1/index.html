Unit 1: Programs and Purpose

Write your first p5.js programs: draw on the canvas, add color and motion, respond to the user, and debug. Files are also in the Unit 1 Google Drive folder.

Learning goals

  • Place shapes on the canvas using x, y coordinates.
  • Explain how drawing order and color functions affect what appears.
  • Choose between drawing shapes from the corner or the center.
  • Use variables to animate shapes.
  • Display text and respond to mouse and keyboard events.
  • Find and fix errors in a sketch.
